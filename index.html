GPS-Denied Drone

Learning to keep a multirotor stable when navigation gets difficult.

An RL stabilization framework for multirotor drones operating in GPS-denied environments. PPO was trained over 600k timesteps in Unreal Engine 5 and AirSim to suppress rotational instability and inertial-navigation drift under stochastic wind disturbances.
